# The Whoville Ledger

Oh, look. A Who who reads the *source*.

Of course you do. You write everything down, and then you go and read
how the writing-down was made. Exhausting. Every one of you.

Here's what this is: **a notebook.** Somewhere to write your clues,
your ciphers, your coordinates and your crackpot theories. That's all.

**It is not a clue.** There is nothing for you in this repo. No link, no
key, no secret door. Go back to the hunt and stop sniffing my
stationery.

---

### "Can you read my notes?"

*Sigh.* No. Your notes live in **your own browser** (`localStorage`) and
never leave it. Nothing is sent anywhere, and there is no server to send it to.
Clear your browser data and they're gone. Use **Export** if you want a copy.

What happens on the page, stays on the page.

Mostly.
